● Reference Guide

Where Does Dashboard Data Come From? (Simple Column Guide)

A simple, easy-to-read reference explaining exactly which Excel file, sheet, and column each number on the dashboard is taken from.

📁 1. How the Data is Organized

Every district has an Excel file in the /data folder. Here is how they work:

Step 1

District Excel File

Each district has 1 main Excel file (for example 5Dataset_kota_Oct26.xlsx for Kota).

Step 2

Block Sheets

Inside each file, there is a separate sheet for every Block (e.g. SANGOD, LADPURA, ITAWA).

Step 3

Village Rows

Inside every sheet, each row represents 1 revenue village with its area, population, and land details.

Step 4

Dashboard Display

When you select a district, the dashboard sums up these rows and displays the cards, map, and charts.

📊 2. Top Summary Metric Cards (KPIs)

Where each of the 6 main cards at the top gets its numbers:

Dashboard Card Excel Column Column Name in Excel How it is Calculated
1. Total Land Area Column F Total Geographical Area Sum of village areas in Hectares from field survey. Subtitle shows block count & village count.
2. Common Land Area Column O + Column P Pasture (Col O) + Potential CFRR (Col P) Total community commons recorded in Excel: Permanent Pasture (Gauchar) plus Potential CFRR Area.
3. Pasture Land (Gauchar) Column O Permanent Pasture & other Grazing Land Area Official pasture grazing land in Hectares recorded for each village in the Excel workbook.
4. Rural Population & Households Column H & Column G Total Population and Total Households Total human population (Col H) and number of families/households (Col G).
5. SC & ST Population Column K & Column M SC Population + ST Population Total marginalized community population (Scheduled Caste Col K + Scheduled Tribe Col M) and percentage share of rural population.
6. VBGRAMG Works Done Column AB Work done under VBGRAMG/ other Govt schemes Total Hectares of land restored or developed under VBGRAMG/government schemes, and number of villages with works.
NRM 1. Intervention Plots Columns AB, AC, Q Works Narration & Scheme Rows Count of field intervention plots actively tracked from village rows having recorded restoration actions or CVS committees.
NRM 2. Demarcation & Bylaws Column W & Column V Demarcation of Pasture land (Yes/No) & Bylaws Number of villages where pasture boundaries have been officially demarcated and village resource bylaws adopted.
NRM 3. CVS Formed & Tenure Column Q & Column T CVS Resolution & Tenure Recognized under 170(1) Number of villages having formed Charagah Vikas Samitis (CVS) and total Hectares recognized under Rajasthan Tenancy Rule 170(1).
NRM 4. VBGRAMG & Encroachment Column AB & Column Y Work done under VBGRAMG (Ha) & Encroachment removed (Ha) Total Hectares of commons restored under VBGRAMG alongside Hectares of pasture land successfully cleared of encroachment.

🗺️ 3. Interactive Map & Work Markers

Where map boundaries and colored dots come from:

Map Element Source / Column What it Shows
Village Boundary Lines (Black outlines) Observatory GeoServer WMS (Census 2011) Official village polygon boundaries matching the village Census Code from Column E (CODE11).
GPS Plot Locations (Colored dots) Column AD & Column AE Latitude (Col AD) and Longitude (Col AE) recorded during field survey.
Work Name & Type Column AC (Activities narration) Description of work (e.g. Talab/Pond digging, Charnot pasture restoration, plantation).
Work Area (Hectares) Column AB (Work done under Govt schemes) Plot size in Hectares restored or developed under government schemes.
CVS Committee Status Column Q & Column R Shows whether Charagah Vikas Samiti (CVS) community committee is formed (Yes/No).

📈 4. Interactive Charts & Data Provenance (All 6 Charts)

Detailed breakdown of every chart series, dual axes, formulas, and exact Excel column sources:

Chart 1: Land Resource Composition (Donut Chart)
Nested Donut / Sunburst

Breaks down total district or block land into 4 distinct categories. Slices show Hectares and percentage share.

Visual Series / Slice Excel Column Excel Column Header Calculation & Formula Tooltip Details
Pasture (Gauchar) Column O Permanent Pasture and other Grazing Land Area Sum of pasture land in Hectares for all villages in selected district/block. Area in Ha and % share of total geographical land.
Potential CFRR Commons Column P Potential CFRR Area (Ha) Sum of potential Community Forest Resource Rights land in Hectares. Area in Ha and % share of total geographical land.
VBGRAMG Restored Land Column AB Work done under VBGRAMG/ other Govt schemes (Ha) Sum of land Hectares where restoration works have been implemented. Area in Ha and % share of total geographical land.
Other Village Land Area Column F minus (O + P) Total Geographical Area minus (Pasture + CFRR) Formula: Geo Area - (Col O + Col P). Represents non-commons land (private agriculture, abadi settlements). Remaining village acreage and share of total.
Chart 2: Block-wise Common Land Distribution
Stacked Bar Chart

Ranks blocks from highest to lowest common land acreage, stacking Permanent Pasture and Potential CFRR.

Visual Series / Segment Excel Column Excel Column Header Calculation & Formula Tooltip Details
Permanent Pasture (Bottom Stack) Column O Permanent Pasture and other Grazing Land Area Sum of pasture land in Hectares for all villages in each block. Pasture Land: XX ha
Potential CFRR Area (Top Stack) Column P Potential CFRR Area (Ha) Sum of potential CFRR area in Hectares for all villages in each block. Potential CFRR: YY ha
Total Bar Height (Sort Order) Column O + Column P Pasture + Potential CFRR Total Common Land = Col O + Col P. Blocks sorted in descending order of total commons. Total Common Land: ZZ ha (XX.X% of block area)
Chart 3: Social Equity & Marginalized Population
100% Stacked Bar Chart

Visualizes the social and caste composition of rural population by block across 3 stacked categories totaling 100%.

Visual Series / Stack Excel Column Excel Column Header Calculation & Formula Tooltip Details
Scheduled Caste (SC) Share Column K & Column H Total SC Population & Total Population Formula: (Sum of SC Population in Block / Sum of Total Population in Block) * 100 SC Population: XX (XX.X%)
Scheduled Tribe (ST) Share Column M & Column H Total ST Population & Total Population Formula: (Sum of ST Population in Block / Sum of Total Population in Block) * 100 ST Population: YY (YY.X%)
General & OBC Communities Share Derived (Col H - K - M) Total Population minus (SC + ST Pop) Formula: 100% - (SC % + ST %). Represents non-SC/ST rural demographic share. General & OBC share percentage.
Total Vulnerable Population Column K + Column M SC Population + ST Population Combined marginalized population count and % of block rural population. Header alert: Vulnerable: XX.X%
Chart 4: Gender Balance & Sex Ratio
Benchmark Bar Chart

Monitors demographic gender equity across blocks, showing females per 1,000 males alongside policy benchmark lines.

Visual Element Excel Column Excel Column Header Calculation & Formula Tooltip & Threshold Details
Block Sex Ratio (Bar) Column J & Column I Total Female Population & Total Male Population Formula: (Sum of Female Pop / Sum of Male Pop) * 1000 Color-coded bars: Green (≥925), Amber (905–924), Red (<905). Shows Female & Male counts.
District Average Benchmark Dashed Line District-wide Female & Male Totals District benchmark reference line set at 922 females per 1,000 males. Benchmark marker line: District Avg: 922.
Critical Alert Threshold Dashed Line State Policy Alert Threshold Fixed reference threshold line at 900 females per 1,000 males. Critical alert line: Alert: 900.
Chart 5: VBGRAMG Restoration Works by Block
Dual-Axis Combination (Bar + Line)

Combines restored acreage on the left axis with the count of active villages on the right axis for each block.

Visual Series / Axis Excel Column Excel Column Header Calculation & Formula Tooltip Details
VBGRAMG Restored Land (Ha) [Left Axis Bars] Column AB Work done under VBGRAMG/ other Govt schemes (Ha) Direct sum of all restored or developed land Hectares recorded for every village in the block under VBGRAMG. Work Done Area: XX Ha
Villages with Works [Right Axis Line] Column AB & Column AC Work done under VBGRAMG & Activities narration Count of distinct village rows in the block where work has taken place: vbg_ha > 0 or where a work name/narration is recorded in Col AC. Villages with Works: YY (e.g. 33 villages in Pali)
Total Block Villages (Context) Row Count Total rows in block sheet Total number of revenue villages surveyed in the block (e.g. 111 villages in Bali). Total Block Villages: ZZ
Chart 6: CVS Formation & Pasture Governance
Multi-Bar Comparison Chart

Compares three key community pasture governance milestones across blocks side-by-side.

Visual Series / Bar Excel Column Excel Column Header Calculation & Formula Tooltip Details
CVS Formed Villages Column Q & Column R CVS Resolution (Yes/No) & CVS Date Count of villages where a Charagah Vikas Samiti (CVS) committee has been established (marked 'Y' / 'YES' or valid formation date recorded). CVS Formed Villages: XX
Pasture Demarcated Villages Column W Demarcation of Pasture land (Yes/No) Count of villages where pasture boundaries have been officially surveyed and demarcated on the ground (marked 'Y' / 'YES'). Pasture Demarcated: YY
Village Bylaws Passed Column V Bylaws (Yes/No) Count of villages where community rules and bylaws for grazing management and pasture conservation have been enacted (marked 'Y' / 'YES'). Village Bylaws Passed: ZZ
